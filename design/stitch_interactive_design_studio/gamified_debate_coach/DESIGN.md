# DESIGN SYSTEM — ARGUMENTA (DokterDebat)

## Brand Identity & Theme
- Brand: Argumenta — AI Debate Coach & English Speaking Trainer
- Theme: Duolingo-inspired energetic gamified learning with serious competitive debate credibility and original identity.
- Maskot: Kiko si tokek hijau-toska lincah dengan ekor gelembung bicara.

## Color Palette
- primary: #20B87A (Lively Teal-Green — brand coaching & primary actions)
- primary-dark: #174E43 (Deep Forest — headers, dark anchors, contrast text)
- primary-light: #E8F8F1 (Soft Mint tint for active pills, cards)
- secondary-accent: #D9F58A (Fresh Lime — milestones, celebrations, badges)
- background: #F9FAF3 (Warm Cream — softer than pure white)
- surface: #FFFFFF (White cards & surfaces)
- surface-subtle: #F1F4EC (Warm subtle gray-green for unselected states/borders)
- accent-orange: #FFB14A (Streak flame & XP rewards)
- accent-purple: #8668E8 (Advanced challenge & Pro badge)
- accent-coral: #EF6D6D (Record indicator, errors, warnings)
- text-main: #1D332C (Dark slate green)
- text-muted: #6C7973 (Soft forest gray)
- border: #DDE7DE (Clean soft borders)

## Typography
- Titles/Headings: 'Nunito Sans', -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, sans-serif, font-bold or font-black, friendly rounded feel.
- Body/UI: 'Inter', -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, sans-serif, legible at 14–16px.
- Numerical/Scores: Bold, high-clarity tabular figures for timers (07:00) and scores (78/100).

## UI Shapes & Controls
- Border Radius: 16px to 24px rounded corners (rounded-2xl to rounded-3xl).
- Buttons: 3D-ish tactile buttons with a subtle bottom shadow/border (e.g. border-b-4 active:translate-y-1 active:border-b-0).
- Badges: Pill shapes with bold microcopy.
- Bottom Nav: 5 persistent destinations (Beranda, Latihan, Coach, Progres, Profil) with clean icons and active teal pill indicator.
- Floating states & feedback chips for debate mechanics.
